## Signals worth knowing
- New channels do break out in cosmic scale: e.g. a 48-day-old channel (11k subs) had 6.7M and 1.5M views.
- Realistic baseline is much lower: a 22-day-old 1.7k-sub channel posting cosmic comparisons gets ~30-80k per video.
  Base rate 8.3% means roughly 1 in 12 small-channel videos reaches 50k. Reaching 3M cumulative views is
  hit-driven; nobody can promise it.
- Crowded: many near-identical titles ("Universe vs Infinity", "Largest vs Smallest"). Differentiation needed.
- Winning durations: median 44s, range 12-60s. Titles are "X vs Y" / "How Many Fit Inside?" style questions.
